Questions, answered short.

What is base-transaction-decoder?

An API that turns any Base mainnet transaction into plain English and strict JSON: what moved, who it touched, risk flags with evidence, and which checks could not run.

Do I need an API key or an account?

No. Every client gets 50 free calls every 24 hours, counted per one IPv4 address, or one IPv6 /64.

How do I pay for more calls?

A $9 pass for 10,000 calls over 30 days, by card or in USDC on Base, or $9 a month for the same calls by card, renewing. Agents can also pay $0.02 per call in USDC on Base via x402.

Are the risk flags a safety guarantee?

No. A flag means evidence was found. The absence of a flag is not a safety guarantee.

Is there a language model in the response?

No. The decode is deterministic, and fields that depend on an outside lookup say whether it ran.

Which chains are supported?

Base mainnet only.

More detail is in the docs.
