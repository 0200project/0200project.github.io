Very little to attack.

No private keys and no accounts. The service holds very little, by design.

No private keys

The service never asks for, receives or stores keys or seed phrases.

No accounts

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics.

Payments

USDC payments settle on Base through x402, verified by a facilitator that can confirm a payment but cannot move or redirect funds. Card payments run on Stripe’s hosted checkout, so card numbers never reach us.

No model in the answer

Every response is a deterministic decode of public chain data.

Read-only blocklists

The ScamSniffer and MyEtherWallet lists are read, never written. How fresh each one is is published on /healthz.

Honest limits

A flag means evidence was found. A missing flag is not a safety guarantee, and nothing here is financial advice.
