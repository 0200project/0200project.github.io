After delivery

Once the work is delivered, we send a payment request for the agreed amount. Nothing is due before.

USDC on Base by default

USDC on Base is the default because the on-chain transfer is the receipt, and the receipt is the invoice. That transfer is public on Base. Card can usually be arranged by email, and if your wallet shows a warning when you sign, stop and use card.
